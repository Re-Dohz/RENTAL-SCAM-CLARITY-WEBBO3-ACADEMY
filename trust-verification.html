Trust and Verification

Before You Pay,Verify

Scammers can copy photos, create convincing profiles,
and impersonate legitimate agents. Our verification
system gives you clearer information before you make a decision—not a promise that every transaction is risk-free.

Know who you’re dealing with before you pay.

✓ Our goal is to collect evidence and not false certainty.

How Verification Works.

Verification is a process, not a promise.

Each check gives you another piece of evidence to consider before making a rental decision.

Identity Check

We collect and review the identity information required from agents, landlords, or property representatives.

Contact Verification

We check whether the contact information associated with the account matches the information submitted during verification.

Property Information Check

Property information submitted by a lister is reviewed for consistency and obvious warning signs.

Community & Report History

Previous reports, complaints, and verification activity may contribute to the trust information shown on a profile or listing.

Important:Verification badges do not mean “100% safe.” They tell you what information we have checked—not what we can guarantee.

TRANSPARENCY

What verification can—and cannot—do.

What we can promise

✓ Clear verification status

We will clearly indicate whether available information has been verified, is pending, or has not been verified.

✓ Transparent information

We will tell you what a verification badge actually means.

✓ A reporting channel

If something looks suspicious, you can report it to us.

✓ Review of reported concerns

Reports can be reviewed and investigated according to our platform's procedures.

✓ Accessibility

Trust and safety information should be understandable and usable regardless of how someone navigates the website.

What we cannot promise.

× We cannot guarantee every landlord is honest.

× We cannot guarantee every property exists exactly as advertised.

× We cannot replace physical inspection or independent verification.

× We cannot guarantee that every scam will be detected before it happens.

×We cannot replace physical inspection.


Verification reduces uncertainty. It does not eliminate risk.

Safety Check List

5 things to check before sending money

Inspect the property

Whenever possible, visit the property yourself or send someone you trust.

Confirm who you're paying

The name of the person or business requesting payment should make sense with the person you've been dealing with.

Ask for documentation

Request relevant tenancy, ownership, or representation documents where appropriate.

Be suspicious of pressure

“Pay now or lose the property” is a warning sign.

Don't rely on a badge alone

A verification badge is one piece of information—not proof that a transaction is completely safe.

“Something Went Wrong?”

Think you've encountered a scam?

Don't send more money. Save your evidence and report the problem as soon as possible.

Our platform cannot recover money or replace law enforcement or legal advice.
