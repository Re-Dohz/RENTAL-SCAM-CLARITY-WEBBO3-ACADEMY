THE PATTERN

How it usually happens

Rental scams often follow a familiar pattern. A renter sees a property, builds trust with an agent and is pressured to pay before properly verifying the person or property.

01

A property is advertised

A seemingly attractive property appears online, through social media, messaging platforms or a personal referral.

02

Trust is created

The person presenting the property may appear professional, provide pictures or claim to represent the owner.

03

Pressure to pay begins

The renter may be told that other people are interested or that the opportunity will disappear if payment is delayed.

04

Money changes hands

Inspection, agency, legal, caution or rental payments may be requested before the renter has completed proper verification.

05

Communication breaks down

After payment, the agent may become unreachable, the property may not exist or the renter may discover that the person was not authorised to offer it.

!

The risk is real

Lagos State has advised residents to verify the registration of real-estate agents and developers with LASRERA before concluding transactions.

Verify before any money moves.

WATCH THE PAYMENT POINT

Common fees demanded

Different charges can appear during the rental process. The presence of a fee does not prove that an agent is legitimate.
