The rental scam problem in Lagos

Finding a home should not mean risking your savings. Unverified agents, misleading listings and pressure to pay can turn the search for accommodation into a costly and stressful experience.

How it usually happens

1

A property is advertised

A renter sees an attractive property through social media, messaging apps, referrals or an unverified listing.

2

Trust is created

The person presenting the property may use convincing photos, professional language, referrals or claims of property ownership.

3

Pressure to pay begins

The renter may be asked to pay inspection, agency, legal, caution, deposit or rent fees before proper verification.

Warning Signs

Be extremely cautious if the person refuses to meet at a physical office, demands cash or personal peer-to-peer transfers, or rushes you before viewing documents.

Always cross-check agent identities using our directory before making financial commitments.
